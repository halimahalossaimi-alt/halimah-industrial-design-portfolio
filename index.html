About me

Designing useful things for real life.

I am a second-year Master of Industrial Design student at Arizona State University, graduating in May 2027.

My background in digital marketing, graphic design, and applied exercise science helps me look at design from different points of view. I enjoy turning research and observations into products, spaces, and experiences that feel clear, thoughtful, and easy to use.

I am especially interested in product development, human-centered design, wellness and healthcare, outdoor recreation, home products, and micromobility. My process includes sketching, CAD, prototyping, rendering, and testing.
